100% Quality & Freshness Guarantee

At TomorrowNeeds, our goal is to deliver produce that meets the highest standards of farm freshness. If any item does not meet your expectations, we resolve it without friction.

1

Doorstep Inspection & Instant Rejection

You have full freedom to inspect your delivery in the presence of our delivery partner. If any fruit, vegetable, or grocery item is damaged, overripe, or missing, simply reject that specific item on the spot.

2

2-Hour Post Delivery Window

In case you notice any quality discrepancy after delivery, you can report it via the app or WhatsApp support within 2 hours of receiving your package. Share a quick photo of the item, and our team will issue an instant refund.

3

Instant Wallet Refund

Refunds for accepted claims are credited immediately to your TomorrowNeeds Wallet with zero deduction. Wallet credits can be used on your next purchase or transferred to your bank account on request.

4

Order Cancellation

You may cancel an order free of charge at any point before it leaves our packing hub. Once dispatched for transit, cancellation requests are evaluated based on delivery partner transit status.

This Privacy Policy explains how TomorrowNeeds collects, uses, stores, and protects your personal information when you access our mobile and web applications.

1. Information We Collect

When you register or place an order on TomorrowNeeds, we collect:

  • Personal Contact Details: Name, mobile number, email address, and delivery coordinates.
  • Location & Address Information: Delivery address, postal code, and saved landmark tags for accurate routing.
  • Transactional History: Orders placed, payment status, wallet balance, and support communication records.
  • Device & Usage Logs: App version, browser type, and navigation flow to optimize app performance.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use your data solely to fulfill orders and deliver a superior customer experience:

  • Processing, packing, and doorstep delivery of your orders.
  • Sending real-time order status, delivery notifications, and billing invoices.
  • Managing wallet rewards, cashback bonuses, and referral perks.
  • Providing proactive customer care and resolving queries promptly.

3. Data Protection & Non-Disclosure

We do NOT sell, rent, or trade your personal data to third parties.

Your payment data (cards, UPI) is processed securely via PCI-DSS compliant payment gateways (Razorpay). TomorrowNeeds does not store raw credit card numbers or banking passwords on its servers.
